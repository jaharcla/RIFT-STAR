# Player guide

1. Begin in the Overworld to unlock **The Fracture Opens**.
2. Follow Blue Skies' native portal route. Entering Everbright unlocks **Prismara**;
   RIFT//STAR does not replace the native entry or return route.
3. Explore Everbright and recover a `blue_skies:diopside_gem` to unlock **A
   Gemstone in Everbright**.
4. Combine the diopside gem, an `ad_astra:steel_plate`, and a
   `minecraft:amethyst_shard` in the Prism Lens recipe. Carrying the result
   unlocks **The First Colour**.
5. Follow the optional threads in any order: visit the Moon through Ad Astra,
   find the Bumblezone through its native entrance, or explore Alex's Caves'
   Primordial Caves. These discoveries add context and never gate the mods'
   own progression.

The `RIFTSTAR_Visuals` resource pack adds a custom prismatic advancement
backdrop. Enable it from the Resource Packs menu for the intended colour
palette. The Prism Lens icon is included with KubeJS assets and loads with the
pack automatically.

## Useful things to bring home

- Blue Skies crystal and midnight glass fit Ad Astra glass components;
  sunstone crystals also light its industrial lamps and glowing pillars.
- Snowcap mushrooms and Alex's Caves frostmint can feed the Ad Astra Cryo
  Freezer. Native ice shards remain the most efficient coolant.
- One guano, one pollen puff and one bone meal make cave fertilizer. Two
  sticky honey residues and a piston make a sticky piston.
- Blue Skies raw foods work with Primordial armor, and cave candies attract
  Bumblezone Honey Slimes. Blue Skies dungeon library chests occasionally
  contain a Primordial cave tablet, which still needs native research.

Every connection is optional. Existing recipes, machines and exploration
routes remain available; JEI and item tooltips show the alternatives.

Client crafting, native travel, and multiplayer acceptance remain manual checks
until a client-capable test environment is available.
